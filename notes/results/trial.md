# Trial: test success per run and between methods

Generated by `python -m analysis.report` from the stored logs.

Success is in points of the 500 test questions, 100 per level; levels 2-4 are their 300 questions. Per token: the mean at 4.00, 4.25, 4.50, 4.75 and 5.00 million generated tokens. Per episode: the mean at steps 100 to 700. Seed 0 is the pilot: it is shown beside the new seeds and left out of every mean and verdict. Differences are computed from unrounded values.

Two windows per episode. The window of the seed is the rule written before the runs: when one run of a seed stops before step 700, every run of that seed is read up to the last multiple of 100 that all of them reached. The window of the pair reads two runs up to the last multiple of 100 that both reached.
